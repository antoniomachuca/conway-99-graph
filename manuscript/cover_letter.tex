\textbf{Subject:} Submission of the manuscript ``Symmetry-Restricted SAT Encodings, Lean~4 Formalization, and Verification Limits for Conway's 99-Graph''
\vspace{0.4em}

Dear Editors,

Please find enclosed the manuscript entitled \textbf{``Symmetry-Restricted SAT Encodings, Lean~4 Formalization, and Verification Limits for Conway's 99-Graph''}, which I submit for consideration for publication in \textit{Experimental Mathematics}.

The manuscript concerns the existence of a strongly regular graph with parameters $\operatorname{srg}(99, 14, 1, 2)$, an open problem on J.~H.~Conway's list of five \$1{,}000 problems. It reports computer-assisted experiments---SAT searches under prescribed automorphisms, DRAT proof checking, and Lean~4 kernel checks---and states what each experiment establishes. It does not decide existence or rigidity. The contents are:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.5pt, topsep=0.5pt]
  \item \textbf{Kernel-checked structural lemmas (Lean~4).} Proofs of $K_4$-freeness, the $7K_2$ decomposition of every neighborhood, diameter at most~2, and a computable checker proved equivalent to the adjacency predicate; the kernel reports only the axioms \texttt{[propext, Quot.sound]}. These facts are standard; the contribution is their mechanization. Arithmetic contradictions from character-theoretic arguments are also kernel-checked; the graph-level wrappers assume their premises and are not reported as proved.
  \item \textbf{A kernel-checked order-7 reduction (Lean~4).} From a Conway 99-graph and a nontrivial automorphism $s$ with $s^7 = \mathrm{id}$, a Lean proof constructs a canonical $15 \times 15$ orbit quotient matrix. It derives the fixed vertex, the orbits, and the quotient equations; it does not assume them. The kernel reports only \texttt{[propext, Quot.sound]}. The orbit structure is known (Behbahani--Lam, Cesarz--Woldar); the contribution is the mechanization. Lean does not prove that no such matrix exists.
  \item \textbf{A checker-verified order-7 CNF refutation.} For one canonical order-7 CNF ($177{,}097$ variables, $422{,}508$ clauses), CaDiCaL~1.9.5 returns \texttt{s UNSATISFIABLE}, and \texttt{drat-trim} reports \texttt{s VERIFIED} on the $26{,}746{,}488{,}053$-byte DRAT proof. Behbahani and Lam (2011) already excluded order~7. The graph-to-CNF translation is not kernel-verified, so the manuscript claims no new proof of that exclusion. It does not decide the CP-SAT model of Thakkar and Severini (2026).
  \item \textbf{Exterior-row CNF, separate from the Lean reduction.} The exterior-row CNF has $26{,}166$ variables and $311{,}703$ clauses. It is not the canonical order-7 CNF, and it is not in the Zenodo record. CaDiCaL~1.9.5 returned \texttt{s UNSATISFIABLE}, and \texttt{drat-trim} reported \texttt{s VERIFIED}. That refutation is \textsc{Proved} for this CNF only. The Lean derivation of the exterior equations is a different \textsc{Proved} component. The implication from \texttt{Z7OrbitMatrix} to the CNF is \textsc{Pending}. The DRAT proof is not checked inside the Lean kernel, and Lean does not prove that no orbit matrix exists.
  \item \textbf{The open cases and search limits.} Every binary $7\times 42$ matrix with $CC^T = 10I_7 + 2J_7$ has column weight~2, and the support labels of the three branch representatives for the involution with one fixed vertex are corrected. A 12.5-day CaDiCaL campaign (more than $2.4 \times 10^9$ conflicts, recorded aggregate) on the open involution and fixed-point-free order-3 encodings found no model and no refutation; it started before a compiler repair. Positive controls apply the production compilers for these two actions to Paley(9) and to the Berlekamp--van Lint--Seidel graph $\operatorname{srg}(243,22,1,2)$. They do not prove compiler correctness. The manuscript also documents a repaired compiler defect.
\end{enumerate}

Every claim carries one of four labels (\textsc{Proved}, \textsc{Compiled}, \textsc{Explored}, \textsc{Pending}) defined in the manuscript. The Lean~4 library, the SAT compilers, and the regression tests are available on GitHub (\url{https://github.com/antoniomachuca/conway-99-graph}). The canonical order-7 CNF and its DRAT proof are in Zenodo record 10.5281/zenodo.22995101.
The solver log, the checker log, and the SHA-256 digests for that CNF are in the same record.
The exterior-row CNF is not in that record.
