Five New Results on Conway's 99-Graph Problem
Elmar G. (elmarguseinov@yahoo.com)
Conway's 99-graph problem asks about the existence of a 99-vertex graph G in which all pairs of adjacent vertices have exactly
one common neighbor and all pairs of non-adjacent vertices have exactly two common neighbors. In 2022-2023, I obtained the
following five results:
1. G is not a subgraph of a strongly regular graph srg(243, 22, 1, 2).
2. G can not be obtained by a generalization of Berlekamp-Van Lint-Seidel's construction.
3. G is Cartesian indecomposable.
4. G contains no Hamming graph H(4,3).
5. The independence number of G is at least 10.
