## Relevance to Conway's 99-Graph

Donald G. Higman established the foundational theory connecting rank-3 permutation groups to strongly regular graphs:
- Every rank-3 graph is strongly regular.
- Higman's parameter relations define the intersection numbers and Krein conditions satisfied by putative graphs such as $\mathrm{srg}(99, 14, 1, 2)$.
- If Conway's 99-graph were to admit a vertex-transitive or rank-3 automorphism group, Higman's theorems would heavily restrict its subconstituent multiplicities. The exclusion of transitive and composite automorphism groups by Behbahani-Lam (2011), Cesarz-Woldar (2025), and the Parity Rigidity Corollary (2026) directly traces back to Higman's framework.
