# Draft emails for external review (to be sent by Antonio; attach discriminant_obstruction.pdf)

## 1. To Dilan Djematene (dilandjematene@gmail.com)

Subject: Your order-3 argument for srg(99,14,1,2): independent check and a generalization

Dear Dr. Djematene,

I read your manuscript "Le graphe de Conway srg(99,14,1,2) n'a pas d'automorphisme d'ordre 3" (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23305123).
I checked the fixed-point-free argument (Lemma 1 and Steps 1–6) step by step and found no error. I also ran the general form of
Steps 1–5 on known strongly regular graphs with fixed-point-free automorphisms; the predicted rank equality and the congruence of
discriminants hold in every case where the hypotheses apply.

The argument extends to any strongly regular graph with integral eigenvalues and any prime p ≤ 19 (working over Z[ζ_p] and using
the norm to Q of the real discriminant). The attached note states and proves this extension and applies it to the open parameter
sets of Brouwer's table: it excludes a fixed-point-free automorphism of order p in 34 cases. Combined with Behbahani–Lam's
computations, it would give that an srg(88,27,6,9) has no automorphism of order 11.

The note has not been reviewed by anyone else. I would be grateful for any comment, in particular on whether you already
considered this generalization. I am also checking independently, by SAT with DRAT certificates, the ±1-valued case of your
Lemmas T16, T18, T20 from the order-2 manuscript; I can send the results when they are complete.

Best regards,
Antonio Machuca

## 2. To Yawara Ishida (yawara@aisr.dev)

Subject: Rank equalities and a discriminant congruence for fixed-point-free automorphisms of strongly regular graphs

Dear Dr. Ishida,

Your paper "No involutions in the missing Moore graph" (arXiv:2606.29183) gives exact rank equalities for prime-order automorphisms.
In the fixed-point-free case, the attached note combines the analogous rank equality with a congruence between the discriminants of
the integral eigenvalue lattices of the quotient matrix and of the cyclotomic matrix (an idea of D. Djematene for srg(99,14,1,2)),
and applies it to the open parameter sets of Brouwer's table.

The note has not been reviewed. I would be grateful to know whether part (a) of the theorem is a special case of your Proposition 4.4
as I believe, and whether any of the listed consequences is already known to you, in particular the exclusion of automorphisms of
order 11 of an srg(88,27,6,9).

Best regards,
Antonio Machuca
